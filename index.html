    How we work together

    We test on data the model has never seen

    We hold back 900 of our 3,000 transformers until the very end and check them once. That is the only honest way to know if a model works.

    We say when we are unsure

    Every prediction comes with a confidence score or a range. When confidence is low, the answer goes to a person to review.

    We can explain every line

    Each of us can walk through the code and defend the choice behind it. If we cannot explain it, we do not use it.

    Our first project: Transformer Health Check

    Power transformers sit at the heart of the grid. When one fails without warning, the power goes out and the repair is expensive.

    We built two neural networks that read the gases dissolved in a transformer's oil. One names the fault. The other estimates the remaining life in days, with a range. A web app lets anyone try it, and every answer comes with how sure the models are.
